Where it stands

Assumptions

  • The return is earned as a rate: in each scenario the price rises or falls at a constant rate, with volatility on top.
  • A scenario lasts for the period of the table on average, and its end is not known in advance.
  • A sale is not undone, and the proceeds wait in cash.
  • The whole account is in the position.

When the assumptions change

  • With a fixed end date the stop is tighter and moves with time.
  • Under these assumptions a rise in the price is never a reason to sell. A better use of the capital, or a change in the remaining expected return, is a separate question.
  • The volatility is measured from past data. The stop is computed on the assumption that the scenarios entered are right.
